What this version actually does

This is a real, tiny character-level neural language model. It learns to predict the next character of text and then generates new text character by character. Unlike your earlier classifier, it genuinely models text sequences.

Limitation: this is an educational model trained on a small text sample, not a conversational assistant. It may produce misspellings, fragments, or repetitive sentences. It cannot answer arbitrary questions or perform research. A ChatGPT-like assistant requires a much larger pretrained model.

Why the neural model is small

The network uses a trainable character embedding, one hidden layer with tanh activation, and a softmax output. It observes the previous 5 characters when predicting the next one. Training runs by gradient descent with backpropagation. It has none of the scale, attention mechanisms, or vast pretraining data of modern language models.

It needs no libraries, hosted inference APIs, model-weight downloads, or Terminal. Once this experiment works, keep it as your independent neural-network learning project rather than mistaking it for a full assistant.
